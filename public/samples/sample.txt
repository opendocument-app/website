A sample text file
==================

Opened in your browser by OpenDocument Reader. Nothing was uploaded: the
file was read, decoded and laid out on your own machine.

Plain text has no formatting to keep, so this view keeps the one thing it
has - the lines, numbered, in a fixed-width face.

Press the pen above this file. You can then type into it, and the disc
beside the pen downloads what you typed as a new text file. Encoding is
detected on the way in; what comes out is UTF-8.

Now drop a text file of your own onto the page.
